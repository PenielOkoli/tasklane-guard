## Remaining boundaries

Process-local quotas reset across instances/restarts and are not a production-wide budget. Prompt-injection patterns are incomplete and may reject legitimate content. CSP retains unsafe-inline for framework hydration; sanitization plus text sinks provide the output defense. Session cookies and local reviews are a demo isolation model with replay/local editing limits, not authenticated durable persistence. Erasure does not delete downloads or external provider/platform logs, and other open tabs must reload. These limits are visible in the product and documented in the checklist/privacy notes.
